## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: About section blends professional and personal identity
The about section SHALL contain a short paragraph (3–5 sentences) written in first person that introduces Chenyang as a whole person — touching on his engineering work, his love of basketball (Charlotte Hornets), and his interest in music (Animenz, Ne-Yo). It SHALL NOT read as a summary from a CV.

#### Scenario: About text is personal in tone
- **WHEN** a visitor reads the about section
- **THEN** the text feels like a self-introduction to a peer, not a professional summary

#### Scenario: Basketball and music references are present
- **WHEN** a visitor reads the about section
- **THEN** Charlotte Hornets, Animenz, and Ne-Yo are mentioned naturally in context

### Requirement: About section has a clear visual heading
The about section SHALL have a section heading (e.g., "About") styled in Hornets purple (`#1D1160`) using a clean sans-serif font.

#### Scenario: Section heading is visible
- **WHEN** a visitor scrolls to the about section
- **THEN** the heading "About" is clearly distinct from body text and uses the purple accent
